About

A work in progress

Biology is a beautiful and fascinating field, but its fundamental principles are often difficult to comprehend. My goal is to bring together the scattered versions of core concepts into tangible interactions.

Kodolab is free and open source. We welcome contributors!

If you are a scientist, student, or a teacher, I would love your feedback. Anything wrong or missing? What you would like to see? Write to me at mary@kodolab.org.
