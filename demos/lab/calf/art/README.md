# Calf art

Eight frames of Juniper, the calf from [Osmosis Case Study](https://naddicott-dtech.github.io/OsmosisCaseStudy/) by naddicott-dtech, used with his permission. Credit him below the render wherever a frame shows.

Copied from his repo's `public/art/`. His `art/README.md` is the spec: 1200×780 RGBA, calf facing left; `rest-*`, `tonic`, `clonic-*` share a high camera (lying on her side), `sternal` and `standing` a side camera. Alternating `rest-1`/`rest-2` reads as a tremor; `tonic` then `clonic-1..3` is a seizure; `sternal` is recovering, `standing` healthy.
