Myosin V

A motor that walks along actin carrying cargo. Each step is a lever swinging on a head that stays bound to the filament, and the swing is paid for by releasing what is left of an ATP.

  • The second leg is measured. 7PM6 and 7PLU are one construct, chicken myosin Va on actin, by cryo-EM.
  • The first leg is modelled three ways. 4ZG4 is human myosin Vc (— identical), solved without actin; it is placed on the filament by the rigid block that carries its actin face; and its chain stops at the converter, so the lever and light chain past it are 7PM6's, carried rigidly.
  • The in-between frames are interpolated. The lever turns as a rigid body about where it leaves the motor; every other residue turns with its neighbours. Only the three marked states are structures.
  • The nucleotide is what is left of ATP. The pre-stroke crystal holds ADP and vanadate, a stand-in for the phosphate; it is drawn as phosphate. Each leaves by the most open path out of the pocket; the real route is not resolved.
  • One light chain of six. These constructs carry one IQ motif; myosin V's real lever is about six times longer, which is what turns this swing into a 36 nm step.
