  • The numbering is shared. Both entries number in chymotrypsinogen numbering, so the superposition is a match on residue number rather than a sequence alignment, and residue 57 is His57 in both files.
  • Two cuts, not one. Trypsin cuts 15–16 and makes the enzyme; chymotrypsin then excises the dipeptides 14–15 and 147–148 from its own kind. Three chains is the result of the second.
  • Nothing is bound in either file. Water only, so the site is drawn empty in both — which is what lets the two pockets be compared at all.
