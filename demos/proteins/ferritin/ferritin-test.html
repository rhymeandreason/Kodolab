Ferritin

A hollow ball built from 24 copies of one part. Ferritin keeps iron in a form that cannot rust the cell: it oxidises Fe(II) at the way in and stores the mineral inside the shell. Human H chain.

  • The site is the file's own bonds. The iron is CONECTed to Glu27, Glu62 and His65; Glu61 and Tyr34 are the rest of the site and carry no bond to this metal, which is the picture telling the truth about a di-iron centre with one iron in it.
