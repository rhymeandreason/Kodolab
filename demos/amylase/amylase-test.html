α-amylase

The site, not the fold. 1OSE, porcine pancreatic, 2.3 Å. Drag to orbit, wheel to zoom.

Acarbose is a separate molecule, not part of the enzyme: a diabetes drug that lies in the trough where a starch chain would and jams it. Four subsites, six rings, and a nitrogen where one glycosidic oxygen belongs — which is why the enzyme cannot cut it, why it works as a drug, and why it stays put long enough to be crystallised.
