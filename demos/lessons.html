How it works
Kodolab Build

Build your own custom lesson.

Write one sentence, or a whole outline, and a step-through lesson is written from the same library these are made of: cells, organelles, membranes, molecules, proteins. It takes about five minutes and is free. You edit it in words, every change is a version, and you share it with a link.

Building is included with a teacher account. Email me to request one.

Request a teacher account → Open the builder Beta · free

  1. Write one sentence, or a whole outline

    Where does a tree get its mass from? The Mass of a Tree, above, started as that sentence. An outline with the steps you want works too.

  2. It builds from the library

    The model mounts the same components by name and drives them by parameters. Nothing is drawn from scratch, so the science is the library's, checked once.

  3. Edit, version, share

    Say what to change and the app re-writes. Every edit is kept as a version, it runs in a sandboxed frame, and the link is the app.
